Features

Keep scrolling down, the row moves sideways.

01 · Display

14.2-inch 3K at 120Hz

Colour-accurate at low brightness, so the screen stops glowing like a torch at night.

02 · Battery

18 hours, honestly measured

Real workloads with the browser open, not a looping video at 10% brightness.

03 · Chassis

Unibody shell, 1.19kg

One piece of machined aluminium. No flex under the palms, no creak when you lift it.

04 · Cooling

Two fans, one vapour plate

Sustained load sits at 38 dBA. The fans ramp, but you stop noticing them.

05 · Ports

Two USB-C, HDMI, 3.5mm

Enough to leave the dongle at home on a normal working day.

06 · Keyboard

1.5mm travel, quiet switches

Built for shared rooms. Tactile enough to type on, quiet enough not to be the loudest thing in the meeting.
